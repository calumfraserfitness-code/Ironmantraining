RACE DAY NUTRITION
PRE-RACE (night before)
High-carb dinner 700–900g carbs total. Pasta, rice, or potatoes. Light protein. No new foods. Bed by 9:30pm.
RACE MORNING (3–4 hrs before)
600–800 kcal · mostly carbs · oats, banana, white toast, sports drink. Coffee if used in training.
SWIM (1.9km)
Nothing. Focus on the swim. Gel in T1 if needed.
BIKE (90km)
60–90g carbs/hour. Aim for 270–360g total. Mix gels, bars, and sports drink. Electrolytes every 45min.
RUN (21.1km)
60g carbs/hour. Gels every 30–40min. Cola at aid stations late in race. Small sips water every km.
